What is Naki Talk 224?

Naki Talk 224 is a free decentralized messaging app that works over Bluetooth mesh — no internet, no servers, and no phone numbers required. Each device acts as a relay, extending the network range so messages can hop from one phone to another, reaching people beyond direct Bluetooth range.

Built for disaster relief, off-grid communities, protests, remote areas, and anywhere internet is unavailable, Naki Talk 224 is part of the NakamaMesh ecosystem, inspired by the humanitarian mission of helping people communicate when infrastructure fails.

Named in honor of the vision to connect the unconnected — 224 representing our community.

Key Features

📡 No Internet Required

Communicates purely over Bluetooth between nearby devices.

🔒 Private & Secure

No phone numbers, no accounts, no central servers to spy on you.

🌐 Mesh Relay Network

Messages hop between devices to extend range beyond direct Bluetooth.

🤝 Cross-Platform

Naki Talk 224 devices can communicate with standard BitChat iOS and Android users.
